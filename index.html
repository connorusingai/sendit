questions

is it free?

Yes. Free to join, post and judge. No ads, and we don't sell your data.

who sees my clips?

Once you post a clip it's public: riders see it in the feed, and anyone you send the link to can watch it. Your email is never shown to anyone.

You can delete a clip, or your whole account, any time in the app. More in Privacy.

how do points work?

Every trick has a value: a 180 is 10, a 540 is 35, a cork 720 is 70. You get the points the first time riders call it landed. A new grab on a trick you already have adds 20%. Repeats pay nothing, so nobody climbs by spamming 180s.

Your pass turns blue at 100 points, black at 300, double black at 600, triple black at 1,000 and park rat at 1,500.

do i have to be good?

No. A straight air is 5 points and a 180 is 10. Everyone starts on a green pass, and bails get posted too.

is it in the app store?

Not yet. Stomped runs in your phone's browser (Safari or Chrome). Add it to your home screen and it opens like any other app.
