Same home screen, five looks

Example data. The content is identical in each one: your rank, trick of the day, what your crew posted and the DROP IN button. Only the art direction changes.

A fits if

you want it to feel like ski culture: edits, park crews, Newschoolers. The game feel comes from the stamp slams, rank-ups and the trophy road.

B fits if

you want more fun and color but still edgy. The rider can come back as a sticker you unlock gear for.

D fits if

you want what Liftoff does: ranks are the reward, and the badges are the art. The Trickgraph gives you a reason to try new trick types.

Either way

A, B and D all show your crew posting on the home screen. That's the part C is missing.
