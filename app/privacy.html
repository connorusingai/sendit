Privacy

Effective October 14, 2026

The short version. We keep what the app needs to work: your login, your profile and the clips you choose to post. Clips are public once posted. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you across other sites. You can delete any clip, or your whole account, from inside the app.

Who can use Stomped

You must be at least 13. If you're under 18, check with a parent or guardian before posting videos of yourself.

We don't use your location. The camera is only used while you're recording, and only after you allow it. Recordings stay on your phone until you choose to post them.

Stored on your phone

The app saves a few settings (like ski or snowboard), a copy of the app so it opens with no signal, and any clips you recorded without signal until they upload. These stay on your device. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Trick recognition

We're building software that recognizes tricks from video, so clips can one day be checked faster. Clips that have been posted, and the community's verdicts on them, may be used to train and test it. It doesn't affect your points or your clips' results. Clips you remove, or that belong to a deleted account, are not used.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores accounts, clips and videos.
  • GitHub Pages serves the app.
  • Open-Meteo provides the snow report. We send it the location of the mountain you picked, nothing about you.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver fonts and code libraries.

Like any website, these services see your IP address when your phone loads something from them.

Deleting your stuff

  • A clip: tap ⋯ under it, then Remove. The video is deleted; the points you earned stay on your rank.
  • A comment: tap × next to it.
  • Your account: Ranks, then Delete my account. Your profile, clips, videos, votes, likes, comments, friends and duels are deleted right away and can't be recovered.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so in the app before it takes effect.
