Terms of use

Effective October 14, 2026

Ride safe. Film your own tricks. Judge fairly. Be decent. That's most of it. The rest is below.

Skiing and snowboarding are dangerous

Tricks can seriously injure you. Stomped is a game about tricks you choose to do; it never asks you to try something beyond your ability, and points are never worth getting hurt. Follow your mountain's rules and the Responsibility Code, ride terrain parks only where they're open, and don't film while you're riding. You ride at your own risk.

Your account

  • You must be at least 13. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian should know you're using Stomped.
  • One account per person. Keep your password to yourself; you're responsible for what happens on your account.
  • Pick a username that isn't offensive and doesn't pretend to be someone else.

Clips

  • Only post clips of yourself, filmed in the app. Don't post other people's tricks as your own.
  • Get permission before filming people who clearly feature in your clip.
  • You keep ownership of your clips. By posting one, you let Stomped store it, show it to other riders, and use it to improve the app, including training trick recognition, until you remove it.
  • No nudity, violence, hate, harassment, dangerous stunts meant to shock, or anything illegal.

Judging and points

  • Vote on what you see: landed or not, and whether it's the trick named. Don't vote to help friends or hurt rivals.
  • Don't game the system with fake accounts, coordinated voting or recycled clips.
  • Points, ranks and duel ratings are for fun. They have no cash value and can be corrected if something went wrong.

Reports and removal

Any rider can report a clip, a comment or a pass photo, and block any rider. Anything reported by three different riders is hidden. Your pass photo has to be something you're allowed to share: no one else's face without their OK, nothing explicit or hateful. We may remove content or suspend accounts that break these rules, with or without notice.

The app itself

Stomped is provided as it is, while it's still being built. Features may change or stop, and there may be bugs or downtime. To the extent the law allows, we aren't liable for losses from using the app, including injuries from tricks you chose to attempt.

Leaving

You can delete your account any time from Ranks, then Delete my account. See the privacy page for what that removes.

Changes

If these terms change in a way that matters, we'll say so in the app before it takes effect.
