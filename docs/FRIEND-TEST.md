# Friend test: 3 phones, one evening

Goal: find what breaks or confuses people **before** the CU ski club sees it. Not a launch; a rehearsal.
Plan for about an hour, after exam week. No snow needed: film tricks on a trampoline, a curb, a jump off a step.

## Before (Connor, 10 min)
1. Paste `supabase/012_feedback.sql` into Supabase > SQL Editor > Run (switches on the Send feedback button).
   Then do the same with `supabase/013_small_group.sql` (2 votes verify a clip; with 3 phones, 3 was impossible).
2. Open https://connorusingai.github.io/sendit/ on your own phone and post one clip, so the feed isn't empty.
3. Pick 2 friends with different phones (ideally one iPhone, one Android).

## During: give them the link and say only this
"It's an app where you film a trick and other people verify it. Try it, and tell me anything weird."
Then **don't help** unless they're totally stuck. Where they get stuck is the data.

Ask each of them to try, in any order:
- [ ] Sign up and pick a home mountain
- [ ] Film and post one "trick" (a spin, a jump, anything)
- [ ] Judge 3 clips in the swipe judging
- [ ] Add a friend and start a Game of S.K.I. duel
- [ ] Join a crew with your 6-letter code
- [ ] Add it to the home screen
- [ ] Send one piece of feedback (Leaderboard tab > Your profile > Send feedback)

## Watch for (write it down in the moment)
- Where did they pause or ask "what do I do?"
- Anything that broke: camera, upload, buttons that did nothing
- What they tried first without being told
- Did anyone laugh, say "oh that's cool", or show someone else? That's the good stuff.

## After: 3 questions, then stop
1. What was the most confusing part?
2. Would you open it again on the mountain? Why or why not?
3. What's one thing you'd change?

## Then
Drop notes into Connor HQ > Stomped > App > Bugs + feedback, or tell Claude. Claude also reads the in-app
feedback from Supabase. Fix the top 3 problems before showing the CU ski club.
